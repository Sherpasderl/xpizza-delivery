The reframe (La Musa only): big points balance in Playfair, a gold "✓ Ya podés canjear" status chip (shown once the balance covers the cheapest redeemable item; below that a muted "Seguí sumando"), an explicit "Canjeá en el checkout" line that sets the expectation (so the chip is informational — no account-side redemption to build). No money value, no tier ladder, no earn-rate line. X. Pizza mirrors the checkout copy once its card hits 8/8.

Build: display-only, CONFIG-driven (`kind:'points'` renders this card; `kind:'punch'` keeps the X. Pizza card), account.js byte-identical past CONFIG, no money-path. The La Musa `tiers` array can be dropped from CONFIG (or kept unused). Light rewards-domain gate, no codex-money needed.
