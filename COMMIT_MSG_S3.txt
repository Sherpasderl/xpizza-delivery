universal across every assignment writer, so no writer can overwrite another's
claim, and add an always-on sweeper heal that self-recovers any stranded
assignment. The S3 pending-order auto-retry (OFFER pass) ships gated OFF; the
HEAL pass runs every scheduled tick as the safety net for the live-path CAS.
