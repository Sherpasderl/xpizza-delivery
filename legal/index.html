Sherpa Driver — Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 20, 2026 · Last updated: June 20, 2026

English · Español

Sherpa Driver is a workforce application used by delivery couriers of Sherpa S. de R.L. (“Sherpa”, “we”, “us”) to receive delivery assignments and share their live location with our dispatch team while on shift. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how it is handled. The app is intended only for authorized Sherpa drivers, not for the general public.

Background location, in plain terms: While you are clocked into a shift, Sherpa Driver collects your device’s precise location in the background — even when the app is closed or not in use — so the dispatcher can see where you are and coordinate deliveries. Location collection stops when you end your shift or log out. We do not track your location when you are off shift.

1. Information we collect

  • Precise location (including background location). GPS coordinates, accuracy, heading, and speed, collected only during an active shift. Used to display your position to dispatch in real time.
  • Account information. The email or identifier you use to sign in, and your driver name, so we can authenticate you and attribute deliveries.
  • Push notification token. A device messaging token used solely to deliver new-order alerts to your phone.
  • Delivery activity. Orders assigned to you and their pickup / delivery status, to operate the dispatch workflow.
  • Basic device information. Platform and app version, used for technical operation and troubleshooting.

2. How we use your information

  • To show your live location to Sherpa dispatchers during deliveries.
  • To assign orders to you and notify you when a new order is available.
  • To operate, maintain, and troubleshoot the delivery service.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

3. How your information is shared

  • Sherpa dispatch and operations staff — to coordinate deliveries.
  • Google Firebase (authentication, database, and Firebase Cloud Messaging) — our infrastructure provider, which processes this data on our behalf under Google’s terms.

We do not share your data with third parties for their own purposes.

4. Data retention

Live location data is retained only as long as needed to operate dispatch and review delivery operations, and is then deleted or de-identified. Account and delivery records are retained while you are an active Sherpa driver and for a reasonable period afterward as required for our records and legal obligations.

5. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored in access-controlled Firebase services. Location uploads are authenticated with a per-shift token so they cannot be spoofed.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Location is only collected while you are on shift — ending your shift or logging out stops collection.
  • You can revoke the app’s location or notification permissions at any time in your device settings (note this will limit the app’s ability to function).
  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at the address below.

7. Children

Sherpa Driver is a tool for employed/contracted adult delivery drivers and is not directed to children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated “Last updated” date above.
